FROM CLIPS TO STORY

Your first edit, in five steps.

  1. Bring your footage. Add several videos, or drag them into the upload area.
  2. Build your story. Drag clip cards or use arrows. Adjust In/Out times in seconds. Split to remove repeated parts.
  3. Find your rhythm. Choose a style and suggest a shorter cut. Undo changes or reset a clip to its full length.
  4. Make it yours. Add your hook, captions, and optional music.
  5. Preview & export. Keep the tab visible. Download the finished MP4 or WebM.

Videos and edits reset on refresh. The JSON edit plan is a reference only, not a restorable project. Duplicate checks find identical files, not repeated speech. CapCut templates run separately. Supported codecs depend on your browser.
